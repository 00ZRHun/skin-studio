Get to Know Us
A few things you may be wondering

Before your first visit.

Which facial is right for my skin?

Every person’s skin and preferences are different. Tell us about your main concerns and current routine, and we’ll help you explore suitable options based on the treatments we offer.

Do I need to know my skin type before booking?

Not necessarily. Start by telling us what you’ve noticed about your skin and what you’d like help with. We’ll guide you through the next step.

What should I expect during my first visit?

We’ll explain the available treatment, discuss your preferences, and answer your questions before proceeding. Your exact experience depends on the treatment you choose.

How much does a facial cost?

Prices are listed alongside the available services where confirmed. If you’re unsure which option suits you, contact us for help understanding the choices.

How long does a facial appointment take?

It depends on the treatment. Check the treatment details or contact our team to confirm the duration before booking.

Can I book through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use our WhatsApp enquiry link to ask about treatments, availability, and booking arrangements. Our team will confirm the details with you.

Is every facial suitable for every skin type?

Not necessarily. Suitability depends on the treatment and individual circumstances. Tell us about sensitivities, allergies, skin concerns, or current products before proceeding.

What if I’m pregnant or have a skin condition?

Some treatments or ingredients may not be appropriate in certain circumstances. Please tell us before your appointment and seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional when needed.

Do I need to follow an aftercare routine?

Aftercare depends on the treatment. Your practitioner should explain any relevant guidance and when to resume your usual skincare routine.
